## Fixed
[NHL-86]
- `client` The NHL API sometimes return shoddy playoff info.  To wit: one conference finals series will be labelled "Eastern Conference Finals", while the opposite one will simply be labelled "Conference Finals".  We now keep an eye out for this and adjust the names such that whenever one of them contains (Eastern|Western), we make sure the opposite one does too.
- `client` We now make sure that the Western Conference info is always on the left (west) side of the screen, and the Eastern Conference info is always on the right (east) side of the screen.
