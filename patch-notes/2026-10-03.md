## Improved

- The latest date you can choose is now yesterday instead of today.  The other option would be to include today, and poll for new data, since we can't easily tell if games are still ongoing during a live season... this is simpler.
- The text in date pickers and drop-down boxes can no longer be accidentally selected (looks weird) or typed over (none of our lists really benefits from type-to-narrow-down-choices).
- Controls now show the same highlight when you reach them with the keyboard as they do when you hover over them with the mouse.
